## 🎯 How to Actually Use These Resources

**Don't bookmark and forget.** Pick the primary resource for your current week and use it during that week. Don't try to consume everything.

**The daily session:**
1. Open the resource for today's topic (15-20 minutes of reading/watching)
2. Close the resource and try to BUILD something from memory (30-40 minutes)
3. When stuck, return to the resource for a specific answer
4. Write your own explanation of what you learned (10-15 minutes)
5. Commit to GitHub

**The hierarchy of help when stuck:**
1. First, try to figure it out yourself for at least 10 minutes
2. Re-read the day's primary resource
3. Search Stack Overflow for the exact error message
4. Watch a focused video on the specific topic
5. Ask in a community (r/learnpython, Python Discord)

**What NOT to do:** Don't sign up for paid courses on top of these. Everything you need is free. The plan IS the structure. The resources are the content.
